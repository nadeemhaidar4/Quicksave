Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. The short version

QuickSave does not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email, or any profile information. Pasted links are processed in real time and never stored. We show ads from a third-party network to keep the service free.

2. What we process (and don't store)

  • Video links you paste — sent to our server only to extract the media file. URLs and media are held in temporary memory during processing and are never written to disk or logs.
  • Abuse prevention — to stop spam, our server briefly keeps your IP address in memory for rate limiting. These entries auto-delete within minutes and are never sold or shared.

3. Stored on your device only

  • Preferences (e.g. auto-paste on/off) via browser local storage.
  • Recent file names to avoid duplicate download names (auto-cleared after 24 hours).
  • Offline cache via service worker so the app loads fast.

Nothing above ever leaves your device. Clearing your browser data removes it.

4. Permissions

  • Clipboard — only read when you tap "Paste" or enable auto-detect. We never read your clipboard in the background.
  • Downloads — files you choose to download are saved to your device's Downloads folder.

5. Advertising

We display ads via the Monetag ad network to keep QuickSave free. The ad network may use cookies or device identifiers to show relevant ads and measure performance. We do not control their practices — please review Monetag's privacy policy for details.

6. Donations

Donations are processed entirely by UPI apps or PayPal. We never see or store your payment details.

7. Third-party platforms

QuickSave can process public links from Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and X (Twitter). We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any of these platforms. Their own privacy policies apply to your activity on their services.

8. Children's privacy

QuickSave is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of QuickSave after changes means you accept the updated policy.
